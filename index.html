HOMESICK TONIC, BEACH TROPICS, & TRANQUILITY

Inspired by my emotions of intense homesickness and excitement for the end of my spring semester, I created an experience to convey the moments of my summer vacation that not only bring me joy but also help calm my mind after months of hard work.

While my piece shares a lighter tone, its origin is rooted in how difficult it can be for me to stay strong on my own, even as my loved ones cheer me on from 635 miles away.
